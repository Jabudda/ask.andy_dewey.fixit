A day at the office

6:00 AM The scheduler rings. Dewey grabs the binoculars.

DEWEYDo we... fit it? Remote? Hires in Wisconsin? Pays $50k or more? Into the pile you go.

DEWEY, MUTTERINGSeen it. Seen it. Duplicate. Expired in 2019. Seen it.

DEWEYSkills 25, requirements 20, remote 15... This one wants a "Synergy Ninja Rockstar" and says nothing else. Thin posting. Capped at 74.

DEWEYAnd this one? Internal candidate, lateral move. Title alone doesn't decide it. Seventy-seven. Recommended.

REALITY: A 0 to 100 score, explained line by line, using evidence only from Andy's verified profile and master resume.

10:30 AM The Writing Room. Howe rolls up his sleeves.

HOWEHow do we fix it? Every line gets a receipt. E0.11, K48, A11. No fact card, no sentence.

HOWEAnd the last letter got rejected. Let's learn something. Lead with results, skip the excuses.

REALITY: Opt-in Claude writes from numbered facts, cites each one, and can learn from an earlier unsuccessful application.

REALITY: The claim checker blocks uncited lines, numbers that aren't in the facts, and skills Andy hasn't verified.
